V9a page marks: signed off

Signed off by claritise, 2026-09-29 (the answers are at the end). V9a is the first part of page-annotations.md: A1 marks under words (solid = not saved, particles included; dotted = tracked or learning; none = fresh, known, ignored or suspended) and A3 the side buttons stepping only between marked words while a card is open. A2’s page stats and A4’s seen-again dot were dropped. Nothing here calls Lexirise: the marks come from V7b’s page analysis (cached on the SD card) and V7a’s copy of the vocabulary. Screens are the X4 Pro’s 480×800 pixels, drawn from the firmware’s own geometry and font bitmaps.

1 · A Japanese page

The reader at its defaults (NotoSerifCJK 14 pt, Normal spacing, justified, extra paragraph spacing, the status bar with battery, chapter title and progress). The marks are drawn over the page after it renders, from the page’s cached analysis (which word is where) and the vocabulary copy (what you know), so the text doesn’t move. The made-up reader here has a young vocabulary, so most words are unsaved and underlined.

3 · A Chinese page

The same rules; the reader’s font is the same NotoSerifCJK family (Japanese letterforms, slimming question S1).

4 · Stepping with the card open (A3)

Today the side buttons step to the previous or next word of the sentence, then on through the page’s next sentences (popup-ui.md §3.3). With A3 (on by default) they step only between marked words (solid or dotted), across the page: a fresh, known, ignored or suspended word is skipped. Past the page’s last marked word a press stops. The card itself doesn’t change. On a page the analysis hasn’t reached there are no marks, so the buttons step every word as today. With a young vocabulary nearly every word is marked, so A3 skips little.

5 · Settings and the reader menu

Two rows in Settings → System → Lexirise, in a new group after General; the web page’s Lexirise section shows the same rows, as it follows the device’s. For one book, the reader menu gets one row that turns the marks and A3 off for that book (kept like its Lookup language). These two screens are approximate (the list’s spacing isn’t re-derived pixel for pixel).

6 · Pages without an analysis

A page is marked only once V7b has analyzed it, which happens only while WiFi is already up (claritise’s “only if already on”): the next page is analyzed while you read this one, for about 5 minutes after a card (wifi_idle_min), and every analyzed page is kept on the SD card. A page without an analysis looks exactly like frame A (and C): no marks, and the side buttons step every word. It stays plain until the next turn (no extra refresh when its analysis lands).

What changes

  • Marks drawn over the page after it renders (A1): no reflow, no layout change, no new font.
  • The side buttons, with a card open, skip unmarked words (A3). The card draws nothing new.
  • Settings: a group of two rows. Reader menu: one row per book.
  • New strings: On the page, Mark words on the page, Side buttons on a card, Marked words, Every word, Page marks.
  • Refresh: a marked page costs nothing extra (the marks are drawn with the page). A save or level change on the card shows on the page when the card closes (the page is redrawn then anyway).

The answers (2026-09-29)

  1. New words, claritise: “Every unsaved word”. A1 as written: every word not saved (or at level 0) gets the solid underline, particles included.
  2. The seen-again dot (A4), claritise: “Drop it”.
  3. The status bar, claritise: “Keep the chapter title”. No page stats (A2 draws nothing).
  4. A3, claritise: “Marked words by default”. A Settings row switches back to every word; past the page’s last marked word a press stops.
  5. The rest (the recommended defaults, decided by the coordinator): the radio stays “only if already on”; the underline 2 px at baseline + 6, dotted 2×2 every 4 px; a page not analyzed yet stays plain until the next turn; settings: the two global rows above and the per-book Page marks row.
