Parked, not signed off (2026-09-29): claritise dropped A5 and parked A10 and A11 (page-annotations.md §2 “Maybe later”). Kept as the record. Was proposed for sign-off. V9b is the second part of page-annotations.md’s page annotations: A10 a recap of the words you looked up, chapter by chapter, with Save all; A11 look up later, for words you tried to look up while offline; and A5 marking only words above your level. A10 and A11 share one list. A5 is measured below and recommended to drop. Screens are the X4 Pro’s 480×800 pixels in the firmware’s fonts.

1 · The looked-up words list (A10 and A11)

One new row in the reader menu opens a list of the words you looked up in this book, the chapter you’re in first, then earlier ones. A word is listed once per chapter: every word you tapped to open a card (not the words you stepped through with the side buttons), and every word you tried to look up while offline (§3). Ignored words aren’t listed. Each row shows the word, its reading and first meaning (kept on the SD card when the card showed them), and its state now, from the vocabulary copy. Rows are for reading: tapping one does nothing in V9b.

2 · Save all

Save N asks first (the list can hold many words, and an Undo for all of them would be two calls a word). Then each unsaved word is saved as a card save is (the word’s dictionary form, its first meaning, the sentence it was in as the note, your tags and the book’s tag, so it lands in the book’s deck), at tracked, one after another behind a progress popup like Sync Vocabulary’s. It’s your own request, so it may join WiFi. Any button stops it. A waiting word is looked up first. A saved word’s level can still be changed from its card.

3 · Look up later (A11)

The page annotations spec says “offline, long-press flags the word”, but a long-press already opens the card (or the offline dictionary). The proposal needs no new gesture: a lookup Lexirise couldn’t answer (offline, a timeout, the rate limit) is kept as waiting, with its sentence, and nothing new is drawn at the time (the card or the offline dictionary shows as today). A waiting word is looked up later, only while WiFi is already on (as the page analysis: after a card brought it up, one word at a time while you read), or at once from the list’s top row, which may join WiFi (your own request). Looked up, it shows its reading and meaning in the list.

With that, the ⋯ tab’s Look up later row (drawn by the approved card, today “Not in this version yet”) has nothing left to do: a word you can see the card for is already looked up and listed. The proposal removes the row (a change to the approved card, so it’s asked).

4 · Above-level marks (A5): measured, recommended to drop

A5 would mark only words above a target level (N2, HSK 4). Measured read-only on 2026-09-29 (tools/lexirise/probe_v9b.py, lexirise-api-notes.md “Levels for A5”):

  • The page analysis still carries no level. Only a dictionary lookup does, one word per call. A novel-like page has 105–129 different words, a manga page ~43; even with repeats, a word’s first lookup costs more calls an hour than the key allows next to the page analysis.
  • The vocabulary copy could keep the level for free (each saved word carries it), but A5 is about the words you haven’t saved.
  • Many words have no level at all: 35% of a Japanese page’s words (particles, and compounds like 港町, 坂道) and 22% of a Chinese page’s (老街, 店铺, 屋檐).
  • The word’s frequency rank, which the page analysis does carry, is a poor stand-in for JLPT (a rank cut agrees with “above N3” for 74% of a page’s words) and a fair one for HSK (91–92%).

So an honest A5 can’t say “above N2”. What’s possible for free is a frequency option, shown here for comparison: unsaved words among the language’s most common ~3,000 aren’t marked. On the probe’s pages that leaves 39 of 105 and 42 of 129 words marked. It goes against “Every unsaved word” (V9a), so the recommendation is to drop A5.

Questions for claritise

  1. A5 (above-level marks): drop it, or build the frequency option (L, M) instead? Recommended: drop. No cheap source of JLPT/HSK levels for unsaved words; a frequency cut is a different feature and undoes “Every unsaved word”.
  2. Where the recap lives: a reader-menu row only (B), or also shown by itself when you finish a chapter? Recommended: the menu row only. Nothing pops up between chapters.
  3. Which words are listed: the words you tapped, or also the ones you stepped to with the side buttons? Recommended: tapped only (stepping through a sentence would fill the list, and Save all would save them).
  4. Save all: ask first, save at tracked, no Undo (D–F)? Recommended: yes.
  5. Look up later: keep every lookup Lexirise couldn’t answer as waiting (no new gesture), look them up only while WiFi is already on or from the list’s row, and remove the card’s Look up later row (I, J)? Recommended: yes.
  6. The wording: “Looked-up words” for the menu row and screen, and the strings above? Recommended: as drawn.
