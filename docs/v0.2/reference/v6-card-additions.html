1 · Session counter (C1) and vocab count (C7): where they show

What: 3 saved · 11 looked up for the session (since the book was opened; reset on close or sleep; RAM only) and 1,204 words in Japanese (C7: languageCount, one GET /v1/vocabulary?limit=1 per session, then counted up and down locally with each save and Undo). The question is where.

Touch

A: nothing new: the rank row stays one target (▼ opens the detail view). B: the summary box goes at the next tap anywhere, or with the home screen's next redraw; no timer (a timer on e-ink is an extra refresh just to erase it). It's shown only when the session looked something up. Sleep ends the session without a summary (the sleep screen is CrossPoint's, and nobody sees a box drawn as the reader sleeps); a chapter end gets none either (C1 suggested one there: it would be an extra refresh in the middle of reading).

Strings

StringWhere
3 saved · 11 looked upnewA: the rank row. B: the summary's first line
1,204 words in Japanese / in ChinesenewB: the summary's second line (left out when the count never came, e.g. offline all session)

Fit

A: the rank row has about 150 px left after ▮▮▮▯▯ #29,774 rare and ▼. 3 saved · 11 looked up just fits; 12 saved · 140 looked up next to a five-digit rank doesn't, so it would be cut or dropped on exactly the busy sessions it's for. B: the box is the toast's frame and type (§1.1: 3 px frame, 17 px text), two lines instead of one.

Recommendation: B, the book-close summary only

The card stays pixel-identical to the approved one (subtract, don't add): the counts are about the session, not the word, and the rank row is the word's. At the close they're a small reward, with both numbers together. Definitions: saved = saves that were kept (words and sentence cards; an Undo takes one back); looked up = cards opened by a tap on the page (a tap on another word counts; side-button steps don't, so skimming a sentence doesn't inflate it). The C7 count is fetched once, on an idle card (as V3's deck steps are), never at the close. Build cost: a hook in CrossPoint's home screen, which draws the box once as it opens. To measure before building: whether languageCount counts sentence cards too (C7's open check).

2 · The “also” reading (C15, C10 option 1)

What: dictionary/lookup's multipleReadings lists other readings. analyze/text reads 一日 in 四月一日 as ichinichi; the lookup lists tsuitachi (the right one here), ichijitsu, tsukitachi, hitohi, ippi (measured, lexirise-api-notes.md). The card doesn't choose (V10 will, from the sentence); it shows the others so the right one isn't hidden. Chinese: 长 in 长得 is read cháng, but here it's zhǎng.

Touch

None new. The Japanese reading line is already a target (kana ⇄ romaji), and popup-ui.md §1 already says the toggle switches "the ‘also’ readings" with the rest; the target grows with the line. Chinese: no toggle, as now.

Strings and rules

StringWhere
· also newbetween the card's reading and the other readings; , between those
  • Order: the lookup's own transliteration first when it isn't the card's reading (tsuitachi here), then alternatives in Lexirise's order, each once, never the card's own reading.
  • Fit: whole readings only, as many as fit before T L F K (226 px); when some don't, the line ends in …, the card's usual cut. In kana, one alternative fits (いちにち · also ついたち…); in romaji the same (ichinichi · also tsuitachi…).
  • When: phase B, with the badge (it comes from dictionary/lookup). A word with no other readings draws exactly the approved card.

To measure before building: whether Chinese lookups return multipleReadings for polyphones like 长, and in which form (tone marks or numbers). The Chinese screens assume tone marks.

Recommendation: A, on the reading line

It sits where the reading is read, in furigana order, and the approved spec already expects "also" readings on it (§1's kana ⇄ romaji toggle). B moves nothing but puts a reading on the grammar line, far from the reading it qualifies, and Chinese has no surface form to lean on. Neither adds height.

3 · Save the sentence as a card (C3), and C17's “Save sentence” row

What: the ⋯ tab's second row, Save the sentence as a card, is in the approved card already (today it answers "Not in this version yet"). Saving is POST /v1/vocabulary with mode: "sentence" and an explicit proficiency. The X4 Pro is touch-only (D15), so C3's ▲▼ grow/shrink becomes two touch targets.

Touch (B)

  1. Tap Save the sentence as a card: the ⋯ tab's body shows the sentence (the Examples tab's type: 21 px, 「」, the word underlined), then Shorter | Longer, then the same row again, which saves.
  2. Shorter drops one clause (split after 、，；, and ， ； in Chinese): the first clause while there's one before the word's, then the last. The word's own clause always stays. Longer puts back the last clause dropped; with none dropped, it adds the next clause on the page (a sentence D5 cut inside a quote), never past the page (D5: a sentence doesn't cross one). A tap with nothing to do changes nothing (no grey on e-ink).
  3. Tapping the row saves: Sentence saved as a card · Undo (2 s, as a save's; Undo deletes it) and the body goes back to the rows. Tapping the ⋯ tab, another tab, or a side-button step leaves the preview unsaved.

Strings

StringWhere
Save the sentence as a cardexistingthe row (both steps)
Shorter / Longernewthe two half-width cells (the action rows' frame and type)
Sentence saved as a card · Undoexisting + existing suffixthe toast (the reference's words; the save toast's Undo)

Level: the sentence is saved at learning (sentences default to 2 in Lexirise, so it's always sent). Pixels: the preview uses only the Examples tab's sentence style and the ⋯ rows' style; the half-width cells are the one new shape (two ⋯ rows side by side, 10 px apart).

Recommendation: B, preview with Shorter / Longer

A (one tap saves the sentence as built, the reference's behaviour plus Undo) is the smallest change and needs no new string, but D5's sentence is often a clause too long or cut by the page, and a sentence card is reviewed for weeks; B lets it be fixed first, in the tab that already holds the action, with no new chrome outside the body. If claritise prefers A, B can follow later without changing A's row or toast. To measure before building: what Lexirise does with the same sentence saved twice (a second card, or the first one back).

4 · Un-ignore from the card (C17, a V5 open question)

What: V5's Ignore is a list on the reader (/.lexirise/ignored.ini), never a write to Lexirise. Once its 5 s Undo toast is gone, nothing on the card can take it back; tapping Ignore this word again only says Ignored: won't be marked again. An ignored word looks like any other.

Touch

A: for an ignored word, the row reads Undo ignore; a tap takes the word off the list, shows No longer ignored · Undo (5 s, as Ignore's), and the row reads Ignore this word again. B: the state line under T L F K says ignored after the level (learning · ignored, not saved · ignored); not a target.

Strings

StringWhere
Undo ignorenewA: the ⋯ row, for an ignored word (like Undo save)
No longer ignored · UndonewA: the toast (Undo: existing suffix)
ignorednewB: the state line, after ·

Recommendation: A only, the row reads “Undo ignore”

No new element: one row's words change, where the ignore was done, mirroring Undo save. B puts a word on every ignored word's card for a state that only matters to page marks (V9, not built yet), and crowds the state line; it can be revisited with V9. With A, the plain Ignored: won't be marked again toast for an already-ignored word goes away (the row can't be tapped to ignore twice).

5 · “Save failed” for Ignore: its own words? (C17, a V5 open question)

What: V5 reuses Save failed for an Ignore (or its Undo) the SD card didn't take (6 s), and for a word that can't be listed at all: no entry id and no one-line dictionary form (2 s). An Ignore never saves to Lexirise, so "Save failed" points at the wrong place, and for the key-less word, trying again never helps.

Options

SD write failed (or the card has no list)The word has no key
1 · as nowSave failedSave failed
2 · one stringCouldn't ignore this wordCouldn't ignore this word
3 · two stringsCouldn't save to the SD cardCan't ignore this word

Durations and touch unchanged (no Retry: the next tap on the row is the retry). With 4A, an Undo ignore the SD card didn't take gets the same SD string.

Recommendation: 3, two new strings

Each says what happened and whether trying again can help: the SD card is a thing the reader can check (reinsert, free space), and "Can't" tells them this word never will. Both are toasts in the existing frame; nothing else changes.

What the renderer needs, and what doesn't fit the approved card

Checked against card/CardLayout.cpp, CardMetrics.h and CardModel.h. Everything above uses the card's existing fonts (UiSmall 16.7 px, Ui 20.8 px, the reader family at 8 / 10 / 18 pt) and §1.1's frames; no new font or size.

  • 1B, the summary: drawn on the home screen, not the card, in the toast's frame and type. The card's toast is one line (CardState::toast); the summary is the one two-line box, so the home-screen hook draws it with the toast's metrics (kToastFrame, kToastText, kToastPadV/H, kToastTop) and the same line height.
  • 2A, the “also” reading: kana in ReaderSmall and  · also  in UiSmall on one line, as the surface line already mixes the two; pinyin with tone marks in UiSmall, as cháng is drawn today. The room is the header's left column: 412 − T L F K's 175 − the 11 px gap = 226 px, the same limit fitText cuts the reading at now. The reading's tap area (§1.1) grows with the drawn text. A reading too long to leave room for even one alternative draws as today.
  • 3B, the preview: the sentence is ReaderMedium with the word underlined, as the Context tab's “Met before” sentence; Shorter | Longer are the one new shape: two ⋯ action rows (§1.1's 2 px frame, 11 / 14 padding, 18 px text) side by side with the 10 px row gap between them, text centred and no ›. The body has room for about nine sentence lines at the default size; a longer sentence is cut with … by wrapText's line limit (Longer stops adding when it would be cut).
  • 4A, 5: strings only (a row's words, a toast's words), in frames the card already draws.
  • Nothing asked for here breaks the binding card: with the recommendations, the card view is unchanged (1B), the reading line gains text within its existing box (2A), and the only new element on any card is 3B's pair of half-width rows inside the ⋯ tab's body.

Sign-off: the questions, with the recommended answers

One reply can settle all five ("yes to the recommendations") or name the ones to change. Once signed off, the build updates card-reference.html and popup-ui.md §1.1 in the same commit, and the new states go through the design conformance gate.

  1. Counts (C1, C7): a two-line summary in the toast's frame on the home screen after the book closes (3 saved · 11 looked up / 1,204 words in Japanese); nothing on the card. "Looked up" = cards opened by a tap; "saved" = saves kept.
  2. “Also” reading (C15): on the reading line, いちにち · also ついたち…: the lookup's reading first, whole readings as fit, then …; phase B; the kana ⇄ romaji tap covers it.
  3. Save the sentence (C3, C17): the ⋯ row opens a preview with Shorter | Longer, the same row saves (at learning) with Sentence saved as a card · Undo.
  4. Un-ignore: the ⋯ row reads Undo ignore for an ignored word, toast No longer ignored · Undo; no state word.
  5. Ignore's failure wording: Couldn't save to the SD card and Can't ignore this word.
